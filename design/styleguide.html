The Ring — design system

Layout, density and color relationships follow the WhatsApp desktop client. Token names match src/shell/styles/tokens.css, so values move into the app without renaming. Every color is a light-dark() pair; data-theme on <html> picks the scheme and data-accent swaps the brand color.
